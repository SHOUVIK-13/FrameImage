HELP & FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about using FrameImage, photo security, custom borders, and collage creation.

What is FrameImage?

FrameImage is a 100% free, browser-based photo framing tool built by Angikar Paribar Tech. It allows anyone to add artistic frames, festival themes (such as Sharodiyar Durga Puja and Birthday celebrations), and custom text plates to pictures without installing software or registering an account.

Are my photos kept private and safe?

Yes, completely. FrameImage processes all images 100% locally on your device using client-side HTML5 Canvas. Your photos never leave your device and are never uploaded to any cloud server or database.

How do I add custom text and styled borders to my photo?

Once you select or generate your framed photo, click the Edit button beside the preview to open the custom editing tool. Under the Text tab, type your message, choose your favorite font, adjust the font size, and pick a border style (Sharp, Curved, Wavy, or Neon glow) or a solid Fill plate. You can also drag the text directly on the live preview to place it anywhere.

Can I frame multiple photos at once and download a ZIP?

Yes. Select or drop multiple images into the photo queue. FrameImage will process all photos in bulk. You can preview each result and click ZIP all to download every framed picture in a convenient ZIP archive.

Can I create photo collages?

Yes! Switch to the Collage tab in the frame selector. Choose from Duo (2 photos), Trio (3 photos), or Relief Poster collage layouts. You can assign different uploaded photos to each slot and independently adjust zoom and crop positions.
